## The problem

Portland Film Festival runs at least two classes, panels, or pitch sessions every day of the festival, plus screenings, Q&As, and daily filmmaker coffee chats. That's an enormous volume of footage generated by a team of eight.

Almost none of it gets cut while it still matters. By the time the festival is over, the team is exhausted, the moment has passed, and the material goes cold in a drive.

This pilot fixes that for one festival, at low cost and no risk.

---

**12 vertical social assets**, captioned, brand-consistent, formatted for Reels, TikTok, and LinkedIn.

| Type | Count |
|---|---|

| Sponsor-attributed assets | 2 |

**Four of these are delivered same-day, during the festival**, so you're posting while the event is live rather than three weeks after it ends.

## Why same-day is possible

I work from Syracuse, three hours ahead of Portland. Your evening footage uploads overnight; I cut it between 6 and 9 a.m. Eastern, which is 3 to 6 a.m. your time.

**The assets are in your inbox before your team wakes up.** A local editor attending the festival all day can't turn work around on that clock.

## What I need from you

1. Someone capturing panels and Q&As — a phone on a tripod is sufficient. I'll set up the shared folder.
2. Logo, brand fonts, colors, and social handles.
3. Sponsor list and how each needs to be credited.
4. One point of contact reachable during festival week.
5. Written go-ahead to publish the four same-day assets without pre-approval, or a named person available each morning to approve.

Item 5 matters most. Same-day content dies in approval queues.
