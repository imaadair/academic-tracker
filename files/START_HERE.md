# Start Here

There are ten files in this folder. Ignore all of them for now. Three sessions, about three hours total, and you're live.

---

## Session 1 — Make one clip (90 min)

Everything waits on this. Not planning — a finished clip that exists.

**1. Sign up for Vizard, free tier.** No card. (5 min)

**2. Find a source video.** You need one person on camera, talking, well lit, 20–60 minutes. Browse any of these and grab the first talk that fits:

- `youtube.com/@SaaStr` — B2B SaaS conference talks
- `youtube.com/@HubSpot` — INBOUND sessions
- `youtube.com/@TEDx` — single speaker, immaculate production
- `youtube.com/@WebSummit` — business keynotes

Don't overthink it. Any competent talk works. You're demonstrating format, not curating.

**3. Paste the YouTube URL into Vizard.** You don't need to download anything — these tools ingest URLs directly. Let it run. (10 min, mostly waiting)

**4. Review what it gives you.** It'll propose 15–30 clips. Watch them at 2× and pick the best one. This is the part that's actually yours. (15 min)

**5. Fix it.** Proofread every caption — names and jargon will be wrong. Check it against `Asset_Template_Spec.md`: hook in the first 1.5s, text inside the safe zone, brand at the end not the front. (25 min)

**6. Export. Upload unlisted to YouTube.** (10 min)

**7. Watch it on your phone.** Muted, thumb-scrolling, the way an actual person will see it. If it doesn't hold you for 15 seconds, fix it. (5 min)

**Done when:** an unlisted link exists that you'd be happy for a stranger to judge you on.

## Session 2 — Log 5 prospects (60 min)

**1. Search YouTube:** `[industry] webinar`, sorted by upload date. Rotate verticals — HR tech, cybersecurity, fintech, legal tech, healthcare IT.

**2. For each hit, open the channel and check:**
- Webinars in the last 3 months? ✓
- Shorts tab empty? ✓
- **Presenter visible on camera, not just slides?** ✓ ← the one that matters
- 50–500 employees, real product? ✓
- No in-house video person? ✓

**3. Log it** in `Prospect_List.md` — company, channel, best webinar, and the marketing contact from LinkedIn.

Four are already logged: **HR Cloud, Cerbo, Kilo, eMudhra.** Verify them and add five more.

**Done when:** 9 prospects with named contacts.

## Session 3 — Send 5 emails (45 min)

**1. Pick your best prospect.** Run *their* webinar through Vizard and cut one clip from it. (30 min)

**2. Send that one the custom clip. Send four others the demo clip.** Copy in `B2B_Target_Plan.md`. (15 min)

**3. Log the send dates.** Follow up once on day 5.

**Done when:** 5 emails are out.

## Then

Repeat sessions 2 and 3 weekly. Five emails a week, one custom. Roughly 20 emails gets you a pilot.

**Set the deadline now: 20 prospects logged and 5 emails sent by Aug 24**, when classes start.

## Rules

**Session 1 today.** Not this weekend. The gap between planning and doing is where this dies, and you've done a lot of planning.

**Don't build a website.** Not until someone has paid you. It's the most satisfying way to feel productive without doing anything.

**Don't wait for the clip to be perfect.** It needs to be good, not perfect. You'll make a better one in week three.

**Come back when you have data.** After 20 emails: if zero replies, we fix the email or the clip. After 5 calls with no pilot, we fix the price. Don't grind on a broken step.
