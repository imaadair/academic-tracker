# First Move: HR Cloud

You're right not to burn the trial on a throwaway. So the first clip you ever make is for a real prospect, and it goes out the door.

---

## Who, and why them

**HR Cloud** — HR software (onboarding, engagement, HRIS). El Segundo, CA. Founded 2012.

| Signal | Detail |
|---|---|
| YouTube | **118 subscribers** |
| Recent webinar | **26 views**, 0 likes, 2 months ago |
| Second webinar | 17 views, 1 month ago |
| Runtime | **19:55** — short |
| Content investment | Blog, ebooks, glossary, ROI calculators, a dedicated `/webinars` page |
| Leadership page | Chairman, CEO/CTO, Board — **no marketing exec listed** |

Read that together and the picture is clear: **a company that invests heavily in content marketing, with video as the one channel getting nothing.** They're producing webinars on a schedule and 26 people are watching. That's not neglect, it's a distribution gap.

The short runtime matters practically too — 19:55 is efficient against Vizard's free-tier upload minutes. A 60-minute conference talk would have eaten your allowance for a demo nobody asked for.

**The video:** "Scaling Complexity: Managing Onboarding & Compliance Across Multiple LLCs & Locations"
`youtube.com/watch?v=KRsTjCk4_LI`

## Step 0 — Verify it's usable (2 minutes, do this first)

Open the video. Scrub to 5:00, 10:00, and 15:00.

**Is there a person visible on camera for most of it?**

- ✅ **Yes** → proceed
- ⚠️ **Presenter in a corner, slides dominant** → workable, expect more manual cleanup
- ❌ **Pure screen-share, no face** → **stop.** Switch to Cerbo, or find another prospect. Don't fight it.

I couldn't get the player to load reliably to confirm this myself, so check before you spend an hour. What I could read of the captions is conversational — *"if I'm hired in Arizona and I move to California"* — which sounds like people talking, not slide narration. Promising, not proven.

---

## Step 1 — Find the human (15 minutes)

Do this *before* the clip. If there's nobody to send it to, you've saved an hour.

**1.** Go to `linkedin.com/company/hr-cloud` → **People** tab

**2.** In "Search employees," type `marketing`

**3.** Look for these titles, in order of preference:

| Priority | Title |
|---|---|
| 1 | Content Marketing Manager |
| 2 | Demand Generation Manager |
| 3 | Marketing Manager |
| 4 | Head of Marketing / VP Marketing |
| 5 | Social Media Manager |

Their leadership page lists only three people and none of them run marketing — so this is likely a team of one to three. That's good for you: fewer layers, and the person you find is probably the same person who made the webinar.

**4.** Note their name, exact title, and anything from their recent posts you could reference honestly.

**5.** Work out the email. Common patterns at a company this size:

```
first@hrcloud.com
first.last@hrcloud.com
finitial+last@hrcloud.com
```

Check their `/press-release` and `/contact-us` pages for any published address to confirm the pattern.

**If you can't find an email:** send a LinkedIn connection request with a note instead. For a student reaching out cold, LinkedIn is arguably *better* — it's expected, it shows your face and your Newhouse affiliation, and it doesn't land in a spam filter. Keep the note under 300 characters:

> Hi [Name] — I'm a TV/Radio/Film student at Newhouse. I cut one of your webinars into a vertical clip for LinkedIn, captions and all. Happy to send it over, no strings — is that useful?

## Step 2 — Make the clip (60 minutes)

**1. Sign up at vizard.ai.** Free tier, no card.

**2. Paste the YouTube URL** — `youtube.com/watch?v=KRsTjCk4_LI`. No download needed. Let it process. (~10 min)

**3. Set the output format to 9:16** before generating, if it asks.

**4. Review what it produces.** It'll propose 10–25 clips with scores attached. **Ignore the scores.** They're optimizing for generic virality; you're optimizing for "an HR manager stops scrolling."

Watch them at 2× and pick the one that:
- Is a complete thought, 30–50 seconds
- Opens on a strong sentence — the multi-state compliance pain is the obvious candidate
- Would make sense to someone who's never heard of HR Cloud
- Has a clearly framed speaker

(15 min)

**5. Fix it.** This is where your value lives:
- **Proofread every caption.** Company names, "I-9," "E-Verify," "HRIS," state names — auto-transcription mangles all of it
- Trim the front so it opens on the hook, not on "um, so"
- Check the framing holds through the whole clip
- Check text sits inside the safe zone (see `Asset_Template_Spec.md`)

(20 min)

**6. Export. Upload unlisted to your own YouTube.** Not a file attachment — links don't trip spam filters. (10 min)

**7. Watch it on your phone**, muted, thumb-scrolling. If it doesn't hold you 15 seconds, fix it. (5 min)

## Step 3 — Send it (10 minutes)

> **Subject:** Cut a vertical from your multi-state compliance webinar
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I watched your webinar on onboarding across multiple LLCs and states, and cut a 45-second vertical clip out of it — captions burned in, sized for LinkedIn. It's here: [link]. Yours to post, no strings.
>
> I did it because you clearly put real work into content — the blog, the ebooks, the ROI calculators — and the webinars look like the one piece not getting distribution. That 20-minute recording has probably fifteen clips like this in it.
>
> If it'd be useful to have those produced every month without adding to your plate, I'd be glad to talk. Either way, take the clip.
>
> Ima Kennerly
> Newhouse School, Syracuse University
> [phone]

**Why this works:** the compliment is specific and true — they *do* invest in content, and you can name the pieces. That's not flattery, it's evidence you looked. And "the webinars are the one piece not getting distribution" says the quiet part without ever mentioning 26 views.

**Log the send date.** Follow up once on day 5, then stop.

---

## Then keep going

One send isn't a business. While you wait:

- Verify **Cerbo** (healthcare EHR, webinar at 6 views) the same way
- Add five more prospects
- The HR Cloud clip becomes your demo for everyone else — you now have a portfolio piece *and* a live prospect from the same hour of work

That's the trick you found by refusing to waste the trial: **every custom clip is also your next demo.**
