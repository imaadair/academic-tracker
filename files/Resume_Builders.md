# Résumé Builders — the menu

Ranked by payoff-per-hour for a Summer 2027 development application. All verified as real and currently open.

---

## 1. Festival screening committees ⭐ start here

**This is development work.** You watch submissions, evaluate them, and write recommendations on what should advance. That is the literal job description of a development intern. It's the single highest-value thing on this list and it's free, remote, and self-paced.

It also solves the problem that killed script coverage: watching a 90-minute film is far less punishing than reading a 110-page script, and the judgment you're demonstrating is the same.

| Festival | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Austin Film Festival** | **Open now** | 6,000+ submissions/year. "A few hours per week," flexible weekdays and weekends. Unpaid — you earn a badge or pass by hours screened. AFF is a major festival and it's *writer-focused*, which fits your track exactly. |
| **Sundance 2027** | **Applications open Aug 31, 2026** | Three weeks out. Put it in your calendar now. |
| Santa Fe International | Open now | Festival runs Oct 14–19, 2026 — screening happens before that |
| San Diego International | Open now | Festival Oct 13–18, 2026 |
| Climate Film Festival | Open now | Smaller, easier entry point |

**Apply to three.** They're not exclusive, the workload is self-paced, and the résumé line — *"Screening Committee, Austin Film Festival"* — is recognized by every development exec who'll read your file.

**Effort:** 2–5 hrs/week, seasonal
**Payoff:** Very high
**Do it:** This week

## 2. Sundance Ignite x Adobe Fellowship ⭐ you're a real candidate

Ages **18–25**. **$5,000 artist grant**, a year of Adobe Creative Cloud, the Ignite Lab, and a curated program at the Sundance Festival.

You qualify, and more importantly you have what it requires: **an award-winning short.** "Missed You" won 1st Place at Portland Film Festival's Future Filmmakers Competition. That's a real submission.

Reality check: the 2026 cohort came from **1,100+ applications**. Long odds. But the application itself is reusable — the artist statement and materials feed every other fellowship you apply to.

The 2026 deadline was Feb 12, so expect **~February 2027**. That lands in the same window as your A24 and Blumhouse applications, so plan for a heavy month.

**Also worth applying to:** Film Independent Project Involve, NALIP, Ghetto Film School, Blackhouse Foundation.

**Effort:** 10–15 hrs on the application
**Payoff:** Transformative if accepted, résumé-neutral if not
**Do it:** Start assembling materials in December

## 3. Submit "Missed You" to more festivals

You have an award-winning short sitting still. Every additional laurel is a line, and the work is already done.

Target festivals with student or emerging-filmmaker categories. Submission fees run $30–70, which is the only real cost — budget $200 for six to eight strategic submissions rather than spraying.

**Effort:** 3–4 hours total
**Payoff:** Medium, but the best effort-to-return ratio on this list
**Do it:** This month

## 4. Campus media leadership

Syracuse has genuinely serious student media — **CitrusTV, UUTV, WJPZ, Hill Communications**. A producing or leadership role there is a real credential, and it compounds with the Honors Advisory Board and Black Honors Society roles you already hold.

Aim for a title with *producer* or *director* in it, not general membership. The difference on a résumé is large.

**Effort:** 5–10 hrs/week
**Payoff:** High, but competes directly with everything else for hours
**Do it:** When positions open — usually start of semester

It's in production and listed as expected 2027. **A completed producing credit is worth more than three in-progress ones.**

Nothing new to start here — just protect the time to actually land it. An unfinished film on a résumé quietly raises the question of whether you finish things.

**Effort:** Already committed
**Payoff:** High
**Do it:** Protect the schedule

## 6. The informational interview project

Twenty conversations with development executives, assistants, and producers over a semester. Not networking in the abstract — a documented project with a target number.

Assistants and junior execs answer far more often than senior people, and they're the ones who'll be running things when you graduate. Newhouse alumni are the obvious starting list.

This isn't a résumé line so much as a route to the things that become résumé lines. Most internships at your target companies come through someone who already knows your name.

**Effort:** 1–2 hrs/week
**Payoff:** Indirect but compounding
**Do it:** Ongoing

---

## 7. Remote part-time assistant work

The strongest possible outcome: a remote, part-time assistant or reader role for a producer, manager, or small production company during term.

Hard to land and rarely posted publicly — which is exactly why #6 matters. These jobs come from knowing someone.

**Effort:** 5–10 hrs/week if landed
**Payoff:** Very high
**Do it:** Via the interview project

## The honest tradeoff

**None of this pays.** You started this conversation wanting $2,000/month, and the highest-value résumé work in this industry is unpaid — screening committees, fellowships, festival submissions.

That's a real cost and worth naming rather than glossing. But it's also the actual answer to the question you asked at the beginning: the Summer 2027 internship is worth far more over ten years than a year of side-hustle income, and your applications are already built and staged. The binding constraint is conversion on those sixteen applications, not cash.

If money becomes urgent again, the film school admissions consulting from the reassessment is the fastest honest path — $100–300/hour, season runs November to January, and it doesn't compete with any of the above.
