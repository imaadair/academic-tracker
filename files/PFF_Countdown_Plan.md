## The one thing that can kill this

**Who is shooting, and how does footage reach you?**

Everything above depends on someone in Portland capturing sessions and uploading nightly. No upload, no same-day turnaround, and the pilot collapses into ordinary post-festival editing.

Settle this on the first call, before quoting a price:

1. Are you recording panels and Q&As this year? Who's doing it?
2. What format, roughly what volume per day?
3. Can they upload to a shared folder each night?
4. Who is my point of contact during the festival, and what's their cell?

If they *aren't* recording, don't walk away — ask them to designate one volunteer with a phone and a tripod, and set up the shared folder yourself. A phone on a tripod is completely adequate for vertical social. Solving this for them is worth more than the editing; it's what makes you someone running a piece of their festival rather than a vendor.
