## The pattern across every EDC

Worth stating plainly because it decides whether this model survives:

> **Recent, well-shot EDC content is short and already edited. Their long content is old webinars.**

The ideal source — 30+ minutes, recent, lightly edited, well-framed — barely exists in this category. **CenterState is the exception**, and it's the exception because they run an actual podcast rather than an event-video programme.

Which points at the real target, if you keep going:

> **Organisations that run a video podcast.** Not "companies with webinars." Podcasts are long, recent, talking-head, and produced on a cadence. That's the shape this business needs, and it's a searchable category.
